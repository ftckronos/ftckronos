Outreach is how we bring robotics beyond competition. FTC Kronos represents our team at schools, community events, and alongside other FIRST teams.

## Our goals

- Introduce younger students to STEM, robotics, and FIRST programs
- Run workshops, demos, and hands-on activities
- Support fundraisers and team events that keep the program sustainable
- Build relationships with schools, sponsors, mentors, and new teams

## Get involved

If you would like to collaborate on an event or host our team for a demo, reach out through the contact page on our site. We track our impact throughout the season and share updates here as posts are added.
